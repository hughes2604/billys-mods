# Changelog

## Alpha 0.2 — Powers

Added the Viltrumite Genome, which grants strength, durability, and self-propelled flight while carried. Added configurable flight controls, a four-level forward boost, impact feedback for powerful unarmed attacks, expanded health, adjusted durability, and a Superman glide pose at speed.

## Alpha 0.1 — Elixirs

Added the Elixir of Immortality and Elixir of Mortality, with a dedicated Billy's Mods creative tab.
