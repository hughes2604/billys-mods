# Changelog

## Alpha 0.2 — Powers

Added the Viltrumite Genome, which grants strength, durability, and self-propelled flight while carried. Added configurable flight controls, a four-level forward boost, impact feedback for powerful unarmed attacks, expanded health, adjusted durability, and a Superman glide pose at speed.

Power tuning: empty-handed attacks deal 40 base damage before armor, maximum health is 100 points (50 hearts), and the genome grants Resistance II with 12 armor and 8 armor toughness. Normal flight accelerates smoothly to 1.8 blocks per tick. Boost speeds are 5, 10, 20, and 40 blocks per tick, selected with **[** and **]**; hold **Control** to use the selected setting. Level 3 and 4 boosts trigger a sonic boom ring and sound; boosted flight adds a subtle camera shake. Flight no longer consumes hunger or plays footsteps when skimming the ground.

## Alpha 0.1 — Elixirs

Added the Elixir of Immortality and Elixir of Mortality, with a dedicated Billy's Mods creative tab.
