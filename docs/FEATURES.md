# Features and controls

All items appear in the **Billy's Mods** creative inventory tab.

## Elixirs — Alpha 0.1

- **Elixir of Immortality**: drink to gain immortality. Damage cannot take you below half a heart. Milk does not remove the effect.
- **Elixir of Mortality**: drink to remove immortality.
- Drinking the elixir that matches your current state is blocked to avoid wasting it. The game displays a short message when this happens.

## Viltrumite Genome — Alpha 0.2: Powers

Carry the **Viltrumite Genome** anywhere in your player inventory, armor slots, or offhand to gain its abilities. If you remove it from your carried items, its abilities end.

### Strength and durability

- Unarmed attacks gain greatly increased damage and knockback, with an impact sound, particles, and a brief field-of-view pulse.
- Arrows are deflected, unarmed attacks from other players do no damage, and the genome provides strong general protection.

### Flight

- Press **G** by default to turn Viltrumite flight on or off.
- Hold **Left Control** by default while moving forward to accelerate into a fast boost. The top boost speed is stronger than before, while retaining gradual acceleration.
- Flight follows the direction you are looking. Move forward or backward, and use jump to rise or sneak to descend.
- Flying fast forward engages the Superman glide pose. Slowing down or flying backward returns to the upright hover pose.
- Backward flight is deliberately slower. Flight key assignments can be changed in Minecraft's **Options → Controls → Key Binds** menu.

The key names can be changed in **Options → Controls → Key Binds**. Search for **Billy's Mods** to find the mod controls.
