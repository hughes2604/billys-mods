# Features and controls

All items appear in the **Billy's Mods** creative inventory tab.

## Elixirs — Alpha 0.1

- **Elixir of Immortality**: drink to gain immortality. Damage cannot take you below half a heart. Milk does not remove the effect.
- **Elixir of Mortality**: drink to remove immortality.
- Drinking the elixir that matches your current state is blocked to avoid wasting it. The game displays a short message when this happens.

## Viltrumite Genome — Alpha 0.2: Powers

Carry the **Viltrumite Genome** anywhere in your player inventory, armor slots, or offhand to gain its abilities. If you remove it from your carried items, its abilities end.

### Strength and durability

- Empty-handed attacks deal **40 base damage** (before the target's armor) and have increased knockback, with an impact sound, particles, and a brief field-of-view pulse.
- The genome raises maximum health to **100 health points (50 hearts)**. Equipping it while at full health fills the expanded health bar.
- Durability is powerful but reduced from its earlier version: it adds 12 armor and 8 armor toughness, plus Resistance II. Attacks can still hurt you, though they deal less damage.
- Arrows are deflected, unarmed attacks from other players do no damage, and fall damage is prevented.

### Flight

- Press **G** by default to turn Viltrumite flight on or off.
- Holding forward without boost now accelerates smoothly to a separate cruise cap of 1.8 blocks per tick. It stays well below even Boost Level 1.
- Hold **Left Control** by default while flying forward to activate boost. The four boost speeds are **5, 10, 20, and 40 blocks per tick**; each level doubles the previous level.
- Starting a Level 3 or 4 boost creates a sonic boom ring and sound. Boosting also adds a very subtle local camera shake.
- Press **]** to raise the selected boost level or **[** to lower it. There are four levels. The level changes only the speed while Control boost is active; it does not change normal flight.
- Flight does not consume hunger or play walking footsteps while active, even if you are skimming along the ground.
- Flight follows the direction you are looking. Move forward or backward, and use jump to rise or sneak to descend.
- Flying fast forward engages the Superman glide pose. Slowing down or flying backward returns to the upright hover pose. Backward flight stays slower.
- Flight key assignments can be changed in Minecraft's **Options → Controls → Key Binds** menu. Search for **Billy's Mods**.
